DRISHTI (दृष्टि) — ON-DEVICE AI SENSORY COMPANION
iQOO Hackathon 2026 | Track: Smart Living / Accessibility Tech
Live App: https://drishti-seven-mu.vercel.app
GitHub: https://github.com/M-L-Arjun/drishti-ai

1. THE CORE INNOVATION
Over 40M visually impaired citizens in India face a critical failure with existing assistive tools: cloud vision APIs take 2-5s (causing pedestrian collisions), compromise personal privacy, and fail in offline metro basements. Drishti solves this by running 100% on local smartphone silicon with zero cloud latency and zero telemetry.

2. TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES
* 12-Sector Clock-Face Spatial Radar: Maps the 68° camera field-of-view into clock positions (12 o'clock = straight ahead). Monocular depth separates space into Immediate Hazards (<3ft, instant 200Hz haptic burst) and Navigation Zones (3-8ft, directional audio).
* Medicine Smart-Scan: Edge binarization removes metallic foil glare. Local OCR and clinical regex extract exactly 3 data points: Brand Name, Strength/Dosage, and Expiry Date with instant warning if expired.
* "Who's Near Me" Vector Recognition: Generates 128-d facial landmark vectors stored locally under AES-GCM 256-bit encryption. Raw images are instantly purged. Approaching contacts trigger discreet double-heartbeat haptics and private earpiece whispers.
* Eyes-Free Vernacular UX: Pure OLED black interface (#05060A) controlled by gestures, chassis back-taps, and spatial 3D audio in 7 Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, English, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali).
* Tiered Silicon Engine: Flagship NPU tier runs quantized INT4/INT8 VLMs for deep segmentation; Lite tier runs MobileNetV4 (<70MB RAM) on budget sub-₹15,000 chipsets.

3. IMPACT & VERIFICATION
* Latency: Sub-second on-device reflex vs 3,500ms cloud lag (14x faster).
* Air-Gap Proof: 100% operational in Airplane Mode; zero telemetry.
* 24/7 Permanence: PWA offline caching + Vercel serverless edge CDN.
