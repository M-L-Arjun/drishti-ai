The Critical Barrier

The Cloud Vision Trap

Over 40 million visually impaired citizens in India are failed by existing cloud-tethered aids in 3 critical ways:

⚡

1. Cloud Latency is Dangerous

Cloud vision APIs take 2 to 5 seconds per inference. A pedestrian moving at 1.2 m/s travels 4 to 6 feet in that window. A 2-second delay in street navigation causes physical injury.

Threshold: Obstacle avoidance requires < 200ms
🔒

2. Privacy Vulnerability

Users are forced to stream live video of private bedrooms, confidential ID cards (Aadhaar), bank receipts, and medical prescriptions to third-party commercial cloud servers.

Biometric & spatial surveillance risk
📡

3. Transit Fragility

Real-world transit hubs (metro stations, basement escalators, elevators, and rural bus stops) frequently have zero high-speed mobile data—instantly "blinding" cloud apps.

Assistive tools must never depend on Wi-Fi
Zero-Cloud Philosophy

The Drishti 100% On-Device Architecture

All sensory processing occurs entirely on phone silicon (NPU/GPU/CPU). Zero bytes leave the device.

🧭

1. Spatial Clock-Face Guidance

Translates obstacles to familiar clock sectors (12 o'clock, 2 o'clock) with dynamic distance bands (NEAR < 3ft, MID 3-6ft, FAR > 6ft) and directional linear haptic pulses.

💊

2. Indian Pharma Smart-Scan

Blister-pack OCR isolates drug brand (Dolo 650, Pan 40), Indian pharmaceutical expiry formats (EXP 11/2027), dosage, and dietary advice in local tongue.

⏱️

3. Live Unsimulated Benchmark

Real HTTP round-trip dispatch vs. local NPU stopwatch. Demonstrates 10x-50x speed advantage on stage with cached replay fallback for Wi-Fi-compromised venues.

👤

4. "Who's Near Me" + AES Encryption

128-dimensional biometric embeddings encrypted at rest via WebCrypto AES-GCM-256. Discreet double-heartbeat vibration and whisper when registered contacts approach.

Hardware Diversity: Tiered Models

Assistive tools cannot assume an ₹80,000 flagship. Drishti dynamically adapts to target hardware.

Flagship Class

Standard Tier (NPU)

Target: Snapdragon 8 Elite / Dimensity 9400

  • ✓ Rich Spatial Comprehension: High-density VLM scene descriptors and multi-sector obstacle tracking.
  • ✓ 320x240 Input Resolution: Fine-grained object segmentation and text localization.
  • ✓ Ultra-Low Latency: ~15ms - 35ms on-device inference speed.
Budget / Mid-Range
🚀

Lite Tier (Edge)

Target: Dimensity 6300/7300, Snapdragon 6 Gen

  • ✓ Fast Directional Evasion: Compact quantized models prioritizing rapid hazard clearance over description.

  • ✓ Still Beats Cloud by 10x: Maintains safety-critical response times on sub-₹15,000 devices.
