The Real-World Crisis

The Problem: Why Cloud Vision Fails

Over 40 million people in India live with severe visual impairment. Existing assistive tech fails them in 3 critical ways:

⚠️

a. Cloud Latency is Dangerous

Cloud vision APIs take 2 to 5 seconds. A pedestrian moving at 1.2 m/s travels 4 to 6 feet during that delay. In obstacle avoidance, that delay causes physical collisions and injury.

Obstacles demand instant sub-second response
🔒

b. Privacy Vulnerability

Users are forced to upload live camera feeds of private bedrooms, bathrooms, ID cards (Aadhaar), and sensitive prescriptions to commercial third-party cloud servers.

Severe surveillance & biometric data exposure
📡

c. Connectivity Fragility

Metro basements, rural transit zones, elevators, and stairwells frequently lack high-speed internet. When connection drops, cloud-dependent apps instantly disable and blind the user.

Assistive tools must never depend on Wi-Fi
Fully On-Device AI Companion

The Solution — What Drishti Does

Runs 100% on-device on local phone silicon. Zero cloud dependencies across 4 core capabilities:

🧭

a. Spatial & Scene Guidance

Real-time obstacle narration via on-device vision model mapped to 12 clock-face sectors (e.g. "Obstacle at 2 o'clock, 3 ft") paired with directional linear haptics.

💊

b. Smart-Scan for Medicine & Packaging

On-device OCR + local LLM filters noisy blister packs into just 3 critical data points: Drug Name, Dosage, and Expiry Date, with clear intake instructions.

👤

c. "Who's Near Me" Contact Recognition

On-device face recognition of trusted contacts with zero cloud upload. Discreet double-heartbeat vibration and whispered name when friends approach.

🎧

d. Pure Tactile & Voice UX

Eyes-free by design — no screen dependency anywhere. Intuitive multi-sensory back-taps, double-taps, directional haptics, and natural audio guidance.

Competitive Edge

What Makes Drishti Different

Four uncompromised engineering pillars that set Drishti apart from cloud-tethered aids:

📱

Tiered Model Strategy

Full-quality model on flagship NPUs; fast, lightweight quantized model on budget/mid-range chips (e.g. Dimensity 6300). Works on affordable sub-₹15k phones too, not just flagships.

⏱️

Live, Real Latency Benchmark

Genuine HTTP cloud round-trip vs. on-device NPU stopwatch. Proven live on stage with real network calls—measuring an order-of-magnitude speedup over cloud.

✈️

100% Offline-Verifiable

Works in Airplane Mode with Wi-Fi and cellular disabled live. Proves zero network reliance onstage in front of judges—immune to transit dead-zones.

🔐

Local-Only Face Recognition

128-dimensional facial vector embeddings stored strictly on-device. Encrypted at rest via WebCrypto AES-GCM-256. Never uploaded, never synced, never leaked.
